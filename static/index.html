Dungeons & Dragons 2024

Three tools for picking a character, and for living with the choice

One is for the player who has never rolled a die and does not know what a cantrip is. One rolls the numbers. The last is for the table that has read all of it twice and wants to know exactly what a subclass changes.

Ten questions

Class Finder

Questions about how you like to play, not about game mechanics — how much you want to keep track of, when you start thinking about your turn, which scene sounds like the character you want to be. Ends with a class and an explanation of why. Official and homebrew modes.

Find a class 4d6, drop the lowest

Ability Scores

Roll, then put the number wherever you want — and reroll anything, as often as you want. Standard array and point buy are here too, along with the background increases the 2024 rules attach to your origin.

Roll some dice Every subclass

Subclass Compendium

All the 2024 subclasses plus six homebrew classes and 118 homebrew subclasses for the official ones, each feature listed by level. Hover any rules term for a definition — and a marked term tells you what this subclass specifically does to it. Search with Ctrl + K.

Browse subclasses

All three work on a phone at the table, in light or dark, and none of them needs an account or stores anything about you.
