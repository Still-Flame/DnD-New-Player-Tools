Step 3 · determine ability scores

Roll Your Ability Scores

Roll four six-sided dice, drop the lowest, keep the rest. Put the number wherever you like, and reroll anything as often as you want — nothing here is locked in. The two non-random methods are here too, in case your DM prefers one.

Background — where your origin bonus goes

Rules from the 2024 Player's Handbook: ability scores on p. 38, background ability increases on p. 178. No increase can raise a score above 20.
