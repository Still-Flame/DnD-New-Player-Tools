Which D&D Class Should I Play?

Ten questions, no jargon. Three about how the class plays, four about how you'd like to fight, and three about the kind of person you'd enjoy being. Works the same whether you're joining a long campaign or playing one evening.

A few levels in, every class also picks a speciality — a Fighter becomes a duellist or a knight, a Wizard picks a school. That's a separate decision and it's completely fine to leave it until you get there.
