D&D Tools

D&D 2024, unofficial

Seven tools for picking a character, and for living with the choice

One is a guided walk through the character sheet for the player who has never rolled a die. One finds a class, one rolls the numbers. The rest are for the table that has read it all twice: what a subclass changes, what a spell says, which feat to take, what a longsword costs.

All seven work on a phone at the table, in light or dark, and none of them needs an account or stores anything about you.

Make a Character

Scrolling guide

The 2024 character sheet, one section at a time, with the page staying beside you as you read. Who you are, your six numbers, class and levelling, combat and spells, and an example of play at a table where the players get a few things wrong and the DM sorts them out.

Class Finder

Ten questions

Questions about how you like to play, not about game mechanics: how much you want to keep track of, when you start thinking about your turn, which scene sounds like the character you want to be. Ends with a class and an explanation of why. Official and homebrew modes.

Ability Scores

4d6, drop the lowest

Roll, then put the number wherever you want, and reroll anything, as often as you want. Standard array and point buy are here too, along with the background increases the 2024 rules attach to your origin.

Subclass Compendium

Every subclass

All the 2024 subclasses plus six homebrew classes and 118 homebrew subclasses for the official ones, each feature listed by level. Hover any rules term for a definition, and a marked term tells you what this subclass specifically does to it. Search with Ctrl + K.

Spell Compendium

1,298 spells, 4 books

The 2024 Player’s Handbook alongside Legends & Legacies, Craft and Creation and Lyre’s Guide to Retia, as printed. Filter by class, level, school and book at once, or search the spell text itself. Hover any rules term for a definition.

Feat Catalogue

240 feats, 4 books

Every feat in the 2024 Player’s Handbook, grouped as the book groups them, plus the feats from Legends & Legacies, Craft and Creation and Lyre’s Guide to Retia. Search the text, filter by source and kind, and see the prerequisite and the book page on every card.

Equipment

Weapons, armour, gear

Every weapon with its mastery property, all the armour, tools and adventuring gear from the System Reference Document, and the weapons, firearms and shields from Lyre’s Guide to Retia. Sort by cost or weight, and open a row for the details.
